FAQ

Fair questions

Is this serious?

Completely. The jerseys are real, the league is real, and your logo will really be printed on them. The only fake thing was last season's chest sponsor — and that ran a full season without a single complaint.

How do I actually pay?

Card, through Dodo Payments — we never see your card details. Pick a spot, pay the price shown, and upload your logo on the way back. If you would rather send a bank transfer, email us and we'll sort it out that way.

Bank transfer for now — claim a spot by email, we confirm the price and send you the details. Card checkout is being switched on this week and will take one click.

What does the money fund?

New printed kits for the squad, the league entry fee, and match balls. The €630 goal covers exactly that. Anything above it goes into a better shirt rather than a bigger surplus.

How good is the print, really?

We order the kit once the spots are sold, so the more that sells the better the shirt we can buy. Whatever the budget ends up being, the logos go on as a proper print made to last the season. You approve the artwork before anything goes to the printer, and you get a photo of the finished shirt.

Can two companies share the chest?

The chest is single-occupancy — that's what makes it the chest. Sleeves, hem, nape and back spots are all separate, so bring a friend and take two spots instead.

Will you print any logo?

Almost. Betting, vapes, and anything the league rules wouldn't allow on a pitch are out. Yes, we are aware of the irony. Everything else — startups, kebab shops, law firms, your uncle's company — is welcome.

Who was on the shirt last season?

A logo we put there ourselves. Nobody signed anything, nobody paid anything, and the company still has no idea. That is exactly why the chest is empty this season.
